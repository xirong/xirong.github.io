# Space Texture Credits

## Saturn moons

- `saturn-moons/mimas.jpg`, `tethys.jpg`, `dione.jpg`, and `iapetus.jpg`: NASA/JPL-Caltech/Space Science Institute Cassini and Voyager global mosaics, cropped and resized to 1024×512 by [Orrery](https://github.com/RTF22/Orrery/blob/master/ASSETS.md); that source lists each original and its NASA public-domain attribution.
- `saturn-moons/phoebe.jpg`: NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute, [Cassini global map PIA07775](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/map-of-phoebe-december-2005/), [cropped copy](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Phoebe_map_PIA07775_cropped.jpg); parts without clear coverage remain incomplete.
- `saturn-moons/hyperion-photo.jpg` and `hyperion-observation.jpg`: NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute, [Cassini PIA06245](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/pop-up-moon-non-stereo-version/), ultraviolet image shown in monochrome. The second file is a crop projected onto one side of an approximate ellipsoid; gray represents unobserved areas.

## Small moons of Jupiter

- `jupiter-moons/amalthea-galileo-map.png`: low-resolution partial Galileo SSI mosaic, extracted from the [Amalthea dataset viewer](https://css.earth/amalthea/), which credits NASA/JPL and the PDS Ring-Moon Systems Node. Dark gray marks areas without imaging. This is a partial observation, not a complete global photograph.
- `jupiter-moons/{thebe,amalthea,metis}-observation.jpg`: individual crops from [NASA/JPL/Cornell PIA02531](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/best-images-yet-of-thebe-amalthea-and-metis/), Galileo, 2000.
- `jupiter-moons/adrastea-observation.jpg`: crop from [NASA/JPL/Cornell PIA01076](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/family-portrait-of-the-small-inner-satellites-of-jupiter/), Galileo, 1996–1997. Its low resolution is preserved.
- The two uncropped JPEGs in this directory are the source images. Thebe, Metis and Adrastea's observed-view crops are projected onto one side of a teaching sphere at runtime; gray denotes areas with no image. The projection and sphere shape are illustrative and are not global maps.

## Storm comparison images

- `great-red-spot.jpg`: Jupiter's Great Red Spot in true color, JunoCam observation on July 10, 2017 (PIA21775).
  source: https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia21775-jupiters-great-red-spot-in-true-color/
  credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech/SwRI/MSSS; image processing by Björn Jónsson.
  license: CC-NC-SA, as credited by the source; used for this noncommercial educational project.
- `great-dark-spot.jpg`: Neptune's Great Dark Spot, Voyager 2 observation in August 1989 (PIA00052).
  source: https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia00052-neptune-great-dark-spot-in-high-resolution/
  credit: NASA/JPL.
  license: NASA/JPL public-domain image; attribution retained.

These are photographs of atmospheric features, not planetary surface maps. Applying them to the comparison sphere is an illustrative visualization only. The comparison uses an imagined sphere with the storm's historical long-axis width as its diameter, not the measured volume of a storm.

- `milkyway.jpg`: artist's impression of the Milky Way structure, credit NASA/JPL-Caltech/ESO/R. Hurt, resized locally to 2048px
  source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Artist%E2%80%99s_impression_of_the_Milky_Way.jpg
  license: public domain in the United States for NASA material, with ESO Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 terms noted on the source page
- `miranda.jpg`: Miranda global map, credit JPL/USGS, used as the local texture for 天卫五
  source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Miranda_map_JPL_USGS.jpg
  license: public domain in the United States for NASA/JPL/USGS material, as noted on the source page
